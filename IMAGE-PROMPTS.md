# KDO Premium Site Image Prompts

These prompts produced concept visuals for the V2 public website. They are atmosphere and material-direction images only, not project cases, client endorsements, or performance evidence.

## Shared Direction

- Visual language: premium industrial flooring, low-saturation mineral color, realistic construction/product photography, editorial composition.
- Avoid: obvious AI artifacts, fake text, visible logos, invented client marks, watermark-like labels, exaggerated glossy sci-fi surfaces.
- Use in site: concept visuals must be marked as `concept-visual` and captioned as non-case imagery.

## Home Hero

Prompt: photorealistic wide editorial image of a premium industrial logistics hall with seamless graphite flooring, refined low-saturation color zones in oxide clay, cobalt blue, warm safety ochre and nano sage, precise floor markings, soft natural skylight, architectural scale, no people in close focus, no logos, no readable text, high-end materials brochure photography, 16:9.

Output: `public/media/v2/home-hero.webp`
